# asiamodern — asia-modern (Hallway 25)

## Ratings (1-5, 5 = matches concept at a glance)
- texture_fidelity: 3 — coherent kit but stylized: terrazzo chips are flat solid-color polygons and shoji paper is a single uniform tan; ceiling planks and glazed jars fare better.
- palette_harmony: 3 — warm cream/honey-wood/black is internally harmonious, but the room reads bright golden while the concept is muted grey concrete + charcoal + restrained warm wood.
- architecture_match: 4 — rectilinear portal with wood-lattice transom, wood-slat ceiling with central clerestory skylight grid, track lights, black rails and low plinths all match; concrete piers and recessed niches from the concept are absent.
- decor_props_match: 4 — ceramics on black plinths, black metal rails, and period Asian artworks (Ravi Varma-style oil, Kiyochika-style fireworks print, Bharat Mata figure) are present; concept's box sconces, hanging-scroll formats, and niche vignettes are not.
- entrance_signage: 1 — no "Asia Modern" / Hallway 25 title sign at the entrance threshold; the only legible sign in the room is the far-end exit plaque.

## Defects
- [MAJOR] [signage] No room-title sign at the entrance threshold: entrance_out shows only sconces flanking the portal and entrance_in shows a bare transom — nothing identifies the room as Asia Modern from either side (entrance_out.jpg, entrance_in.jpg).
- [MAJOR] [texture-flat/cartoon] Terrazzo floor chips are oversized, hard-edged, solid-color polygons on a matte beige field with no gloss or fine aggregate — reads as vector confetti, not the concept's polished fine-chip terrazzo; the concept's dark inlaid border lines within the field are also missing (floor.jpg, entrance_out.jpg, exit_out.jpg).
- [MAJOR] [texture-wrong] Upper walls are flat cream plaster with no mottle; the concept's defining grey concrete/plaster material language is essentially absent from the room (all interior views).
- [MAJOR] [lighting] Room is uniformly bright and high-key; the concept corridor is dimmer with pooled museum track lighting and contrast between lit art and shadowed surfaces (entrance_out.jpg, exit_out.jpg, wall_left.jpg).
- [POLISH] [texture-flat/cartoon] Shoji rice-paper cells are one flat tan tone with no paper grain or translucent backlight variation, losing the "screens filter daylight" effect central to the concept (wall_left.jpg, wall_right.jpg).
- [POLISH] [architecture-missing] Lattice screens run full-length on BOTH walls with paintings hung directly on the grid; the concept alternates screens with calm concrete wall zones and recessed niches for art and artifacts (wall_left.jpg, wall_right.jpg, exit_out.jpg).
- [POLISH] [decor-missing] The concept's tall black-framed frosted rice-paper box sconces flanking the portal are replaced by plain beige half-cylinder wall lights; the disc-shaped artifact on a plinth and the console-table-with-scroll centerpiece are also absent (entrance_out.jpg, exit_out.jpg).
- No flipped or mirror-seamed figurative textures found: the three-women oil painting (wall_left.jpg), the fireworks woodblock print (wall_left.jpg — bursts at top, crowd silhouettes at bottom), and the Bharat Mata figure (wall_right.jpg — halo at top, feet on clouds at bottom) are all upright with no horizontal mirror seams; terrazzo and plank tiling show no reflected repeats.

## Hero element
Yes — the long black-gridded clerestory skylight running down the wood-slat ceiling, over full-height shoji lattice walls, is instantly recognizable and matches the concept's signature "wood lattice screens + clerestory glazing" move; the black portal with lattice transom (entrance_out.jpg) is a strong secondary signature. The concept element that would sharpen the hero further is the frosted rice-paper box sconce pair framing the entrance portal.

## Signage
Not at the threshold. Neither entrance_out.jpg nor entrance_in.jpg shows any room-title signage — the portal carries only a lattice transom and two plain sconces. The single legible sign in the room is the far-end exit plaque (visible small in entrance_out.jpg, legible in exit_in.jpg): "STEP INTO THE LIGHT" / "Asia - return to the Grand Crossing" — wayfinding for the exit, not a room title.
