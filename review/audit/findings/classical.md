- texture_fidelity: 2 — wall marble reads as real veined stone, but the dado, frieze, coffers, and mosaic carpet are flat solid-color fills with hard vector edges, not photoreal materials.

## Hero element
Yes — the polychrome coffered ceiling over a fluted colonnade, capped by the red Greek-key meander frieze, gives an instant Greco-Roman read matching the concept's signature. Its impact is diluted by the flat pastel carpet below; making the mosaic carpet read as real tesserae would complete the concept's second hero (the "mosaic central carpet").
