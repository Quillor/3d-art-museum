# americas19 — americas-19th-century (Hallway 05)

## Ratings (1-5, 5 = matches concept at a glance)
- texture_fidelity: 3 — the damask wallpaper has real printed-fiber texture and the floor has believable wood grain, but the wainscot is a flat untextured slab, the floor is the wrong wood pattern, and the ceiling/gilt details are flat graphics.
- palette_harmony: 4 — crimson damask, gold trim, dark wood, warm plank floor is convincingly the right salon family; hue runs salmon/brick rather than the concept's deep oxblood, and the room is brighter overall.
- architecture_match: 3 — coffered ceiling, gilt frieze band, wainscot line, sconce rhythm, and dark portal pilasters are present, but the concept's carved doorway entablature, marble threshold, ornate cornice, and paneled wainscot profiles are all missing.
- decor_props_match: 3 — gaslight-style globe sconces and a proper salon hang of gold-framed era paintings (Washington Crossing the Delaware, The Oxbow, Whistler's Mother, Bierstadt) sell the period, but the concept's settee, bronze bust, and pedestal furniture are absent.
- entrance_signage: 1 — the room has no title sign above its entrance portal (verified from the approach view in nativenorth/entrance_out); the only legible sign in the room is the NEXT room's "THE MODERN ERA".

## Defects
- [MAJOR] (signage) Entrance portal (seen from nativenorth/entrance_out and this room's entrance_in): the lintel face is bare dark wood — no "19TH CENTURY"/room-title sign exists at this threshold, while every neighboring room's portal carries one. Visitors approaching see only "THE MODERN ERA" two rooms ahead.
- [MAJOR] (bare-surface) Wainscot on both long walls (wall_left, wall_right, entrance_out, exit_out; close-up confirms): a completely flat, untextured dark-brown slab with a single gold strip on top — no panel moldings, stiles, or wood grain; concept shows paneled dark-wood wainscoting.
- [MAJOR] (texture-wrong) Floor (floor, wall_left, wall_right): straight strip-plank flooring in honey oak; the concept and README specify varnished herringbone parquet with an inlaid border and aged varnish sheen. Grain is decent but it reads as a modern laminate floor, not a 19th-century salon parquet.
- [POLISH] (architecture-missing) Doorway surrounds (entrance_out, exit_out): plain flat dark pilasters and lintel — no carved wood surround, gilded entablature, or marble threshold from the concept's Salon Entrance Portal panel.
- [POLISH] (decor-missing) No settee, bust on pedestal, or any furniture piece from the concept corridor; the room is walls-and-art only.
- [POLISH] (texture-flat/cartoon) Ceiling (ceiling view): flat white coffer grid with pasted-on flat gold discs as lights (one disc clipped by a coffer beam at the panel edge); concept shows ornamented plaster with discreet downlights.
- [POLISH] (lighting) Overall brightness is well above the concept's dim gaslight mood, and the sconce on wall_left blooms into a blown white hotspot on the wallpaper.
- Directional-texture check: damask motifs (plume fans, medallions) are upright and consistent across the field, the gold frieze band, and the band above it; the pattern's left-right mirror symmetry is inherent to damask design, and no mid-panel horizontal mirror seams or upside-down repeats were found.

## Hero element
Yes. The floor-to-frieze crimson damask with a formal salon hang of instantly recognizable 19th-century American paintings in gold frames, punctuated by globe gaslight sconces, reads as the era at a glance. The concept's other hero — the ornate carved-and-gilded salon entrance portal — is not built; promoting it would complete the room.

## Signage
Not for this room. No title sign is visible or present at the entrance threshold (entrance_out/entrance_in and the approach view all show a bare lintel). The legible signage inside the room belongs to the next room: "THE MODERN ERA" with a small date subtitle, gold on black, above the exit portal.
