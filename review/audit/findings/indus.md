# indus — asia-indus (Hallway 20)

- texture_fidelity: 3 — fired-clay family is right and the floor paving is convincingly weathered baked brick, but wall/ceiling brick is clean, bright and uniformly tiled vs. the concept's dark weathered terracotta with lime-plaster traces, and the drain edges are flat black strips.
- palette_harmony: 4 — cohesive all-terracotta brick palette with dark timber accents matches the concept family, though the whole room reads noticeably brighter/more orange than the concept's deep moody brown.
- architecture_match: 3 — brick corridor with dark timber ceiling beams, pilastered wall bays, threshold step and edge channels all present; missing the concept's plastered display niches and monumental brick portal with timber lintel.
- decor_props_match: 2 — only framed artworks and one small wooden case; the concept's signature lit pottery niches and terracotta seal/relief plaques are absent from every view.
- entrance_signage: 1 — no legible room title sign at the threshold in entrance_out or entrance_in; the only sign in view is a distant unreadable plaque above the far moon gate.

## Hero element
No single instantly-recognizable signature element — the room reads as a competent but generic brick corridor with framed prints. The strongest candidate from the concept sheet is the run of recessed, lime-plastered display niches holding warm-lit terracotta pottery, with the timber-lintel brick threshold portal as a close second; adding either would immediately anchor the Indus read.
