# The Americas 19th Century Hallway Concept Spec

**Period:** 1830-1890  
**Gallery read:** Historicist salon gallery  
**Concept sheet:** `Hallway-05-americas-19th-century.png`  
**Core material language:** damask wallpaper, gilded trim, plaster cornice, marble thresholds, varnished parquet

## High-Level Art Direction
A decorative salon-style corridor with deep wall coverings, gilded picture rails, ornate plaster, dark wood, and warm gaslight-inspired sconces. The sheet is intended as production concept art for a museum subsection: dark editorial board, large cinematic architectural panels, concise captions, and materials readable enough for asset modeling.

## Floor Texture / Material Notes
Use varnished plank or parquet flooring with marble thresholds; avoid plain concrete or generic stone.

Texture priority for Blender materials: damask wallpaper, gilded trim, plaster cornice. Use roughness, bump, and bevels to make the floor catch light without creating unsafe visitor-path geometry.

## Blender Asset-Generation Guidance
Make trim-heavy wall bays with picture rail slots, cornice pieces, herringbone/parquet floor tiles, and ornate door casings. Keep the corridor kit modular and shallow enough to wrap the existing museum path rather than becoming a separate scene. Preserve calm wall zones for artwork, and place ornament as framing, trim, ceiling, floor, or portal detail.

## Suggested Assets To Model

- Brass sconce
