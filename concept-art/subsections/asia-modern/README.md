## High-Level Art Direction
A restrained modern gallery with concrete/plaster, terrazzo, warm lattice or slat screens, translucent panels, and clerestory daylight. The sheet is intended as production concept art for a museum subsection: dark editorial board, large cinematic architectural panels, concise captions, and materials readable enough for asset modeling.

## Floor Texture / Material Notes
Terrazzo, polished concrete, or muted linoleum is plausible; avoid ornate premodern stone floors.

Texture priority for Blender materials: terrazzo, concrete, plaster. Use roughness, bump, and bevels to make the floor catch light without creating unsafe visitor-path geometry.

## Blender Asset-Generation Guidance
Build modern portal frames, floor material variants, wood-screen modules, translucent panel slots, and black metal rails. Keep the corridor kit modular and shallow enough to wrap the existing museum path rather than becoming a separate scene. Preserve calm wall zones for artwork, and place ornament as framing, trim, ceiling, floor, or portal detail.
