**Period:** 500 BCE-1600 CE  
**Gallery read:** Sahelian banco hall

**Core material language:** banco earth plaster, compacted clay, timber toron beams, straw fiber, mineral pigment

## High-Level Art Direction
A sculptural earthen corridor with banco walls, toron beam rhythm, compacted floors, geometric trim, and warm shadow. The sheet is intended as production concept art for a museum subsection: dark editorial board, large cinematic architectural panels, concise captions, and materials readable enough for asset modeling.

## Floor Texture / Material Notes
Compacted clay/earth with straw flecks is plausible; avoid stone or concrete.

Texture priority for Blender materials: banco earth plaster, compacted clay, timber toron beams. Use roughness, bump, and bevels to make the floor catch light without creating unsafe visitor-path geometry.

## Blender Asset-Generation Guidance
Build thick plastered wall modules, toron beam arrays, packed-earth floor shaders, and shallow carved/painted trim strips. Keep the corridor kit modular and shallow enough to wrap the existing museum path rather than becoming a separate scene. Preserve calm wall zones for artwork, and place ornament as framing, trim, ceiling, floor, or portal detail.

## Suggested Assets To Model
- Banco portal
- Toron beam
- Earthen wall bay
- Packed clay floor
- Painted trim

## Runtime Integration Notes
This folder is concept-art only. When these designs are promoted into runtime assets, keep file changes outside this folder intentional and map the materials onto existing corridor segments.
