**Period:** 28,000 BCE-1200 CE  
**Gallery read:** Sandstone rock-shelter gallery

**Core material language:** sandstone, ochre pigment, packed earth, weathered rock, soot, small stones

## High-Level Art Direction
A sandstone rock-shelter gallery with ochre marks, x-ray style motif references, rough floor, and warm concealed lighting. The sheet is intended as production concept art for a museum subsection: dark editorial board, large cinematic architectural panels, concise captions, and materials readable enough for asset modeling.

## Floor Texture / Material Notes
Rough packed earth and rock is correct; keep it matte and uneven but safe for navigation.

Texture priority for Blender materials: sandstone, ochre pigment, packed earth. Use roughness, bump, and bevels to make the floor catch light without creating unsafe visitor-path geometry.

## Blender Asset-Generation Guidance
Create stratified sandstone wall modules, rock-shelter portal pieces, packed-earth floors, ochre motif decals, and small stone clusters. Keep the corridor kit modular and shallow enough to wrap the existing museum path rather than becoming a separate scene. Preserve calm wall zones for artwork, and place ornament as framing, trim, ceiling, floor, or portal detail.

- Ochre motif decals
- Stone cluster
