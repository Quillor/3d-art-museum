## High-Level Art Direction
A warm crafted gallery with carved timber posts, red/black/white painted patterns, woven walls, mat flooring, and clear display zones. The sheet is intended as production concept art for a museum subsection: dark editorial board, large cinematic architectural panels, concise captions, and materials readable enough for asset modeling.

## Floor Texture / Material Notes
Use woven mats or dark timber; avoid stone or concrete.

Texture priority for Blender materials: carved dark wood, pandanus mats, woven reed. Use roughness, bump, and bevels to make the floor catch light without creating unsafe visitor-path geometry.

## Blender Asset-Generation Guidance
Create carved post modules, woven wall and floor panels, rafter bays, pigment trim, and shell/fiber detail pieces. Keep the corridor kit modular and shallow enough to wrap the existing museum path rather than becoming a separate scene. Preserve calm wall zones for artwork, and place ornament as framing, trim, ceiling, floor, or portal detail.
