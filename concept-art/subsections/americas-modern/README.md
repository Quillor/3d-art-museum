# Americas Modern Hallway Concept Spec

**Period:** 1890-1930  
**Gallery read:** Early modern white-cube transition  
**Concept sheet:** `Hallway-06-americas-modern.png`  
**Core material language:** matte plaster, terrazzo, polished concrete, bronze or steel trim, skylight glass

## Online Reference Links Used
- [MoMA: Architecture and design](https://www.moma.org/collection/terms/architecture-and-design)
- [Met: Modern art timeline](https://www.metmuseum.org/toah/ht/11/na.html)

## High-Level Art Direction
A restrained early modern gallery with crisp plaster planes, terrazzo/concrete floors, clerestory light, and minimal metal detailing. The sheet is intended as production concept art for a museum subsection: dark editorial board, large cinematic architectural panels, concise captions, and materials readable enough for asset modeling.

## Floor Texture / Material Notes
Terrazzo or polished concrete is plausible; pale oak or linoleum can be an alternate for specific rooms.

Texture priority for Blender materials: matte plaster, terrazzo, polished concrete. Use roughness, bump, and bevels to make the floor catch light without creating unsafe visitor-path geometry.

## Blender Asset-Generation Guidance
Build modular plaster planes, simple portal blocks, skylight strips, metal rails, and terrazzo floor shader references. Keep the corridor kit modular and shallow enough to wrap the existing museum path rather than becoming a separate scene. Preserve calm wall zones for artwork, and place ornament as framing, trim, ceiling, floor, or portal detail.

## Suggested Assets To Model
- Modern portal
- Terrazzo floor
- Skylight ceiling bay
- Steel trim
- Low plinth

## Runtime Integration Notes
This folder is concept-art only. When these designs are promoted into runtime assets, keep file changes outside this folder intentional and map the materials onto existing corridor segments.
