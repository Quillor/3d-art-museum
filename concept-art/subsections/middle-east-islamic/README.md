# Islamic Golden Age Hallway Concept Spec

**Period:** 700-1500  
**Gallery read:** Geometric tiled gallery  
**Concept sheet:** `Hallway-17-middle-east-islamic.png`  
**Core material language:** zellij tile, marble, carved plaster, wood lattice, glazed ceramic, brass

- [Met: Islamic Art](https://www.metmuseum.org/toah/hd/orna/hd_orna.htm)
- [Victoria and Albert Museum: Islamic Middle East](https://www.vam.ac.uk/collections/islamic-middle-east)

## High-Level Art Direction
A calm geometric gallery with tiled dado, muqarnas ceiling pockets, carved plaster, lattice shadows, and marble floors. The sheet is intended as production concept art for a museum subsection: dark editorial board, large cinematic architectural panels, concise captions, and materials readable enough for asset modeling.

## Floor Texture / Material Notes
Marble slabs with geometric tile borders are plausible; avoid European checkerboard patterns unless specifically localized.

Texture priority for Blender materials: zellij tile, marble, carved plaster. Use roughness, bump, and bevels to make the floor catch light without creating unsafe visitor-path geometry.

## Blender Asset-Generation Guidance
Build arch modules, muqarnas ceiling units, tile dado strips, marble floor borders, and lattice shadow-casting screens. Keep the corridor kit modular and shallow enough to wrap the existing museum path rather than becoming a separate scene. Preserve calm wall zones for artwork, and place ornament as framing, trim, ceiling, floor, or portal detail.

## Suggested Assets To Model
- Ogival arch portal
- Muqarnas ceiling
- Zellij dado
- Marble floor border
- Mashrabiya screen

This folder is concept-art only. When these designs are promoted into runtime assets, keep file changes outside this folder intentional and map the materials onto existing corridor segments.
