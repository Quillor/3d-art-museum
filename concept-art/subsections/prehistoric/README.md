## High-Level Art Direction
A cinematic cave threshold and corridor with natural rock compression, ochre hand marks, animal motifs, and warm museum-safe torchlight. The sheet is intended as production concept art for a museum subsection: dark editorial board, large cinematic architectural panels, concise captions, and materials readable enough for asset modeling.

## Floor Texture / Material Notes
Packed earth with scattered stones is the historically plausible floor. Avoid polished stone, wood, or regular tile.

Texture priority for Blender materials: rough cave rock, ochre pigment, packed earth. Use roughness, bump, and bevels to make the floor catch light without creating unsafe visitor-path geometry.

## Blender Asset-Generation Guidance
Build a low, irregular rock corridor kit with modular wall ribs, eroded ceiling slabs, shallow torch brackets, and clear floor collision. Keep the corridor kit modular and shallow enough to wrap the existing museum path rather than becoming a separate scene. Preserve calm wall zones for artwork, and place ornament as framing, trim, ceiling, floor, or portal detail.
